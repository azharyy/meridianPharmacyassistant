# Delivery

## Delivery area

We deliver to addresses within 10 miles of a Meridian store. Enter your address at checkout to see whether it is in our area.

## Delivery fees

Delivery costs $4.99 per order. Orders of $35 or more are delivered free.

## Delivery times

An order placed by 2 p.m. on a weekday is delivered the next weekday between 9 a.m. and 6 p.m. An order placed later, or on a weekend, is delivered within two weekdays.

## Checking an order

Your order confirmation email has your order number. To check an order's status in the chat, use the order form with the order number and the email or phone number on the order.
