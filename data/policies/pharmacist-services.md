# Pharmacist services

## Talking to a pharmacist

Our pharmacists answer questions about medicines, including whether a product suits you, how it works with your other medicines, and what to do about side effects. Use "Talk to a pharmacist" in the chat, or call your store.

## What the chat assistant can't do

The chat assistant shows what a product's label says. It can't diagnose a condition, recommend a medicine for you, or advise on doses beyond the label. It passes those questions to a pharmacist.

## Medication reviews

Book a free 15-minute medication review at Meridian Downtown to go over all the medicines and supplements you take.

## Emergencies

The pharmacy can't help in an emergency. If you or someone with you may be having a medical emergency, call 911. For a possible poisoning or overdose, call Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222.
