# Returns

## Returning a product

You can return an unopened, unused product to any Meridian store within 30 days of purchase. Bring the receipt or the order confirmation email.

## Medicines that can't be returned

For safety, we can't take back a medicine or eye drops once the package or its seal has been opened. We can't take back a prescription medicine once it has left the pharmacy.

## Refunds

We refund the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving a return. A delivery fee is refunded only when the product arrived damaged or was the wrong item.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an order arrives damaged or holds the wrong item, tell us within 7 days. We will replace the item or refund it in full, including delivery.
