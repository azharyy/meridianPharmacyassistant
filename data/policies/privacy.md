# Privacy

## What we keep

When you place an order, we keep your name, your contact details, the delivery address and the items you bought, so we can fill the order and answer questions about it.

## Order privacy

We show an order's status only to someone who gives its order number together with the email or phone number on the order. The chat never shows the items in an order or its delivery address.

## The chat assistant

Before your messages are processed or stored, the chat assistant removes email addresses, phone numbers and order numbers from them. Please don't type health details you don't want to share.

## Pharmacist requests

When you ask to talk to a pharmacist, we send them a summary of your questions without your contact details. We send it only after you confirm.
